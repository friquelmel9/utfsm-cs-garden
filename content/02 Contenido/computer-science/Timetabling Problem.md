# Timetabling Problem

The Timetabling problem searches for a contamination of schedules and limitations. It needs to find the allocation of recurring events on a schedule to scarce resources across a [[Discrete Set]] of time slots or location spots.

## Model

The timetabling problem can be modeled as an [[Constraint Satisfaction Problem|CSP/CSOP]]
